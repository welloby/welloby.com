Questions and answers

Still stuck? Ask the help bot, or ask in the Discord. The game chat is shown there too.

Ask on Discord

Joining

Is it cracked? Can I use TLauncher?

Yes. You do not need a paid Minecraft account. Cracked (offline) launchers such as TLauncher work, and premium accounts work too.

Every player, premium included, makes a password the first time they join. The password protects your name.

Which versions work?

On Java Edition, versions 1.7.2 to 26.3 work. 26.3 is recommended.

On Bedrock Edition (phone, tablet, console, Windows), use the address bedrock.welloby.com and the port 25620.

How do I join on a phone or console?

Use Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

  1. Choose Play, then Servers, then Add Server.
  2. Enter the address bedrock.welloby.com and the port 25620.
  3. Join, make a password and type /accept.

Some consoles hide the Add Server button. If yours does, ask in #general on Discord. Voice chat works on Java only.

Is there a queue?

A queue forms only when the server is full. There is no whitelist.

When the server is full, you log in, wait on a small platform in the lobby and get in by yourself as soon as a place frees up. First come, first served. If you drop out for less than two minutes, you keep your place. Nobody can pay to skip the queue.

I lost my password. What can I do?

There is no automatic password reset. If you added an email or linked Discord, ask on Discord. That is how you prove the account is yours.

Still logged in? Protect the account now. Type /email add and then your email two times, or type /discord link and send the 4-digit code to the Discord bot.

Forgot your password for this website? Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page.

Where is it hosted and what is the ping like?

In Europe, online 24/7 and protected against attacks. Expect 20 to 60 ms of ping inside Europe and about 80 to 100 ms from the US East Coast.

Playing

Are hacked clients allowed?

Yes, and so is xray. There are no gameplay rules. The anticheat only stops things that break the server.

Does the map ever reset?

No. Not for updates, not for lag, not for any other reason. The map has been running since 6 September 2026.

What do I get when I join?

Five cookies. You start at a random spot, 100 to 1,000 blocks from 0, 0.

What happens when I die?

You drop everything. You come back at your bed if you have one. If not, you come back at a new random spot, 100 to 1,000 blocks from 0, 0.

How do teleports and /kill work?

Teleports cost in-game money and take 10 seconds. /sethome is free, but only in the Overworld within 5,000 blocks of spawn. /home costs $25. /tpa costs $40 and reaches players within 1,000 blocks. There is no /rtp, /spawn or /back.

/kill is free and asks first: type /kill again, or /kill yes, within 30 seconds.

How do I get money?

You start with $200. /jobs join miner pays a little for every block you break, and /vote gives a small reward once a day. /bal shows your money, /pay sends money and /baltop lists the richest players.

How do I find other players?

/lfg posts your coordinates in public chat, once every five minutes. /coords shows your own position above the hotbar. No command shows where anyone else is.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Your friends join with the same address. Then use /tpa to go to them, or meet at coordinates with /coords. On the home page, "Copy an invite for a friend" gives you a message to send them.

Is there voice chat?

On Java, yes: proximity voice chat works with a free mod. Ask in #general on Discord which one to install. Without the mod you can still play, you just do not hear anyone. Bedrock players cannot use voice chat.

Is the Nether roof open?

No. It is sealed at y 127. Highways run inside the Nether.

How do I report a crasher or appeal a ban?

On Discord. Use #reports for crashers and lag machines, with a video clip if you can. Use #ban-appeals to appeal, in one message.

The website and the 3D map

Do I need a website account to play?

No. The game and this website have separate accounts. A free website account lets you use the Community (clans, the forum, messages) and vote on the Server list. Create an account.

I forgot my website password. What can I do?

Open Sign in and choose "Forgot your password?". Type the email on your account, and we send you a link to make a new password.

How do I join or start a clan?

Clans are in the Community. Open Clans, then ask to join one, or start your own. You need a free website account.

How current is the 3D map?

It redraws itself while the server runs. When players build, dig or blow something up, the map draws that part again once the server has saved it, usually within minutes. It covers 5,000 blocks around 0, 0 in the Overworld, plus the Nether and the End near their centers.

Can people see where I am on the 3D map?

No. The map never shows players, and it only covers the area around spawn, so bases far out stay off it.

A mini-game does not load. What can I do?

Spawn Guessr needs the 3D map, so give it a moment on a slow connection, then reload the page. Elytra Dive works without the map. If a game still does not start, tell us in #general on Discord.
