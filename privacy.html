Privacy

This covers welloby.com and its community pages. It's short because we keep very little. Last updated 22 November 2026.

What the community keeps

  • Your account: your nickname, your in-game name if you add it, your email address and, if you add one, a recovery email. Your password is stored only as a salted, scrambled hash, never as the password itself.
  • Sign-in with Google, Discord or Facebook: if you use one, we keep that site's id for your account and the name and email it shares, so the button can sign you back in. We never see your password there.
  • What you post: your profile, posts, reactions, reports, clans and any servers you list.
  • Messages: your private chats, group chats and clan chat. Only the people in a chat can read it. The staff never browse chats: they see a single message only when someone in the chat reports it, and then just that message. Who can start a chat with you, and who you've blocked, is in Settings.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, we keep that device's push address, its two encryption keys and a short name for it like "Chrome on Android", until you turn them off, sign out there or delete your account. A notification travels through the push service of Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your phone and browser. It is encrypted, so they can't read it.
  • Staying signed in: a random code in your browser's local storage. We store only a hash of it, and it runs out after 30 days without use.
  • Spam limits: to slow down floods we count requests per network for a short time. The network address is kept only as a salted hash, and the counters expire.

What your email is for

Confirming the address, sending password reset links, and telling you when your password or email changes. Nothing else: no newsletters, no marketing, and it's never shown to other players or to the staff.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare runs the community server and its database.
  • Resend sends the account emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google, Discord or Facebook, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft, only if you turn on notifications: their push service delivers them to your device.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else. We don't run ads or analytics, and we don't set tracking cookies.

The rest of the site

The server status comes from mcstatus.io. The vote section on the main page shows a MinecraftRanking badge, loaded from minecraftranking.com. Videos load from YouTube or TikTok only when you press play. Links to Discord, Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee and other sites follow those sites' own privacy rules once you're there.

Deleting your data

In the community, open Settings and use Delete account. It removes your name, email, recovery email, linked sign-ins and profile straight away, and takes you out of your group chats. Your posts and messages stay up as "Deleted player" so threads and chats still make sense; you can delete your own messages first, and the staff can remove particular posts if you ask. If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Welloby under Settings and privacy, then Apps and websites, on Facebook. To delete an account you can't get into any more, ask the staff on Discord.
