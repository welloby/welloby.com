The map, in 3D

The real world around spawn, 5,000 blocks out from 0, 0 in every direction, rebuilt block for block from the server's own files. It redraws itself as players build, dig and blow things up. Player positions are never shown.

  • Right-dragto turn
  • Scrollto zoom
  • One fingerto move
  • Two fingersto turn and zoom

The 3D map is a big download, so it waits for you on a data-saving connection.

Open the 3D map

Opens in a new tab. On a phone, use two fingers to turn and zoom.

The Overworld out to 5,000 blocks from 0, 0, plus the Nether and the End near their centers. Coordinates posted on the community forum open right here.

Mini-games

Two small games. Spawn Guessr drops you at a real spot on the live 3D map and you pin where it is. Elytra Dive is a flight under the sealed Nether roof. Best scores stay in this browser.

5 roundsUp to 1,000 points each

Hold the mouse, a finger or Space to lift the nose. Let go and it drops. Climbing spends speed and diving earns it, so dive before you climb. Below 7 blocks a second you stall. Rockets (R) give a burst of speed, and you can pick more up in the gaps. P pauses.

How far is far?

Bases survive by distance. Pick one and see how long the trip takes on foot, on a horse, by elytra or by ice boat, and how much the Nether saves.
