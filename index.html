questions people ask

does the map ever reset?

no. not for a new version, not for lag, not ever. if the world gets big we buy more disk, we do not wipe it. the map started in september 2026.

is it cracked? can i join with tlauncher or an offline account?

yes. offline / cracked names work and so do real mojang accounts. everyone picks a password on first join (/register password password, or the popup on 1.21.6+). there is no email recovery on a cracked name, so do not lose it.

are hacked clients allowed?

yes. there are no rules about how you play - hacks, xray, dupes, griefing, pvp anywhere. the only things that get you banned are lag machines, crash exploits or crash items, and slurs in chat.

which versions work?

the server runs paper 1.21.11 and any java edition client from 1.8 to 26.2 can join through viaversion. very old versions feel a little off in boats and combat. bedrock edition is not supported right now.

where is it hosted and what is the ping like?

london, uk, online 24/7 with ddos protection. europe gets 20-60 ms, the us east coast about 80-100 ms. there is no daily restart.

are there teleports?

yes, but they are local and they cost in-game money. /sethome is free and only works within 5000 blocks of spawn, /home costs 25. /tpa costs 40 and only reaches a player within 1000 blocks. both have a 10 second warmup. there is no /rtp, no /spawn and no /back.

how do i get money?

you start with 200. /jobs join miner pays for every block you break - stone is about 1 each, diamonds about 10. /bal shows your balance, /pay sends some, /baltop shows the richest players. /vote once a day also pays a small reward.

what happens when i die?

you drop everything - hard mode, keepinventory off, no /back. if you slept in a bed you respawn there, otherwise somewhere random 100 to 1000 blocks from 0,0.

is there a queue or a whitelist?

no queue, no whitelist, no ranks, no shop, nothing to buy. add mc.welloby.com and play.

what do i get when i join?

five cookies. that is the whole starter kit. everything else you dig, steal or dupe.

is the nether roof open?

no. the nether roof is sealed at y 127. highways go inside the nether, not on top of it.

how do i report a crasher or appeal a ban?

discord: #reports for crash exploits and lag machines with a clip if you have one, #ban-appeals for appeals, one message.
