Checking the Java server

  1. Add the server. In Multiplayer choose Add Server and paste the address. Any release from 1.7.2 to 26.3 connects, and 26.3 is native. No mods needed. In Play open Servers, choose Add Server, and enter the address and the port.
  2. Pick a password. Cracked accounts set one on the first join. On 1.21.6 and newer a box pops up. On older versions type /register password password, then /login password next time. Premium accounts log in automatically.
  3. Spawn. You land at a random spot 100 to 1,000 blocks from 0, 0 with five cookies. No kit, no tutorial.

Bedrock answers status checks since the 26.3 upgrade, but logging in has not been re-tested yet. If you can't get in, say so in Discord.

          Questions people ask first

          Still stuck? Ask kevzy, the help bot in the corner of this page, or ask in the Discord. Chat in game is mirrored there.

          Does the map ever reset?

          No. Not for updates, not for lag, not for any other reason. The map has been running since September 2026.

          Is it cracked? Can I join with TLauncher or an offline account?

          Yes. Offline (cracked) and real Mojang accounts both work. A cracked name is protected only by the password you register, and there is no email recovery, so keep it somewhere safe.

          Are hacked clients allowed?

          Yes, and so is xray. The anticheat only stops the things that break the server.

          Which versions work?

          The server runs Paper 26.3 with ViaVersion, ViaBackwards and ViaRewind, so Java releases from 1.7.2 to 26.3 connect. Native 26.3 is recommended. Bedrock players (phone, console, Windows) use bedrock.welloby.com with port 25620.

          Is there voice chat?

          Yes. Proximity voice runs through the Simple Voice Chat mod for Fabric, Forge or NeoForge, free on Modrinth. Without the mod you can still play. You just won't hear anyone.

          Where is it hosted and what is the ping like?

          London, online 24/7, DDoS protected. Expect 20 to 60 ms from Europe and about 80 to 100 ms from the US East Coast.

          How do I find other players?

          /lfg posts your coordinates in public chat, once every five minutes. /coords shows your own position above the hotbar. No command shows where anyone else is.

          Are there teleports?

          Local ones, paid with in-game money. /sethome is free but only works in the Overworld within 5,000 blocks of spawn. /home costs $25. /tpa costs $40 and reaches players within 1,000 blocks. Each one takes 10 seconds to fire. There is no /rtp, /spawn or /back.

          How do I get money?

          You start with $200. /jobs join miner pays a little for every block you break, and /vote gives a small reward once a day. /bal shows your balance, /pay sends money and /baltop lists the richest players.

          What happens when I die?

          You drop everything. You respawn at your bed if you have one, otherwise at a new random spot 100 to 1,000 blocks from 0, 0.

          Is there a queue or a whitelist?

          There is a queue only when the server is full. Up to 30 people play at once; when every place is taken you log in, wait on a small platform in the lobby and get in by yourself as soon as a place frees up, first come, first served. Drop out for less than two minutes and you keep your place. There is no whitelist, no priority and no way to pay to skip the queue.

          What do I get when I join?

          Five cookies.

          Is the Nether roof open?

          No. It is sealed at y 127. Highways run inside the Nether.

          How do I report a crasher or appeal a ban?

          On Discord. Use #reports for crashers and lag machines, with a clip if you can. Use #ban-appeals to appeal, in one message.

          How current is the 3D map?

          It redraws itself while the server runs: when players build, dig or blow something up, BlueMap renders that part again once the server has saved it, usually within minutes. It covers 5,000 blocks around 0, 0 in the Overworld, plus the Nether and the End near their centers.

          Can people see where I am on the 3D map?

          No. The map never shows players, and it only covers the area around spawn, so bases far out stay off it.
